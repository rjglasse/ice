# Survey

- `survey-2026.md`: draft of the 2026 post-course survey, item by item, with each item's
  status against the 2025 instrument (repeat / new / dropped) and the 2025 column index.
- The 2025 responses are in `feedback/feedback-y1.csv`; themes in `docs/feedback-2025-themes.md`.
- When the 2026 form is built (Google Forms, as in 2025), export responses to
  `survey/feedback-2026.csv`; the Likert summary in `docs/feedback-2025-themes.md` was
  generated by a short script and can be re-run on both files for the year-on-year table.
